DeepInfer vs vLLM, SGLang & TensorRT-LLM

Heritage, when to choose which stack, feature checklist, and what OSS does not include. Prefill is not a separate product.

Heritage

From the official Acknowledgments: DeepInfer was originally built primarily on vLLM, with additional inspiration from SGLang and TensorRT-LLM. That is lineage — not a claim that every vLLM feature is present.

What OSS does NOT include

  • A turnkey multi-tenant online API with long-retention KV
  • Production Prefill/Decode disaggregation service (phases exist in one engine; full glue is out of tree)
  • DP load balancers for prefill and decode replicas
  • Agent runtime — that is DeepSeek Harness · dshbase.com
  • Protocol conversion alone — that is deepseek-recipe (does not infer)
Prefill ≠ separate product. Prefill and Decode are two phases of the same DeepInfer engine. Charts that show 4× SM100 (prefill) vs 64× SM100 (decode) are topology for benchmarks — not two installs. See Prefill vs Decode.
